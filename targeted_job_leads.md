Here is a recruiter’s insider brief on three distinct, high-growth, remote-friendly pathways tailored specifically to your skill stack. 

As a talent recruiter, I can tell you that tech companies are currently burning budget trying to find "applied" talent. They don't need $300k/year Machine Learning PhDs to build models; they need pragmatic developers who can build reliable bridges between business workflows and existing LLMs using Python. 

Here are the 3 sweet-spot roles matching your exact toolkit:

---

### 1. AI Automation Engineer (AI/Workflow Automation)

### 💼 Job Title & Average Market Rate
* **Target Titles:** AI Automation Engineer, Workflow Automation Developer, Applied AI Developer
* **Average Market Rate:** **$35 – $50/hour** ($75,000 – $105,000/year)

### 🎯 Why Your Skills Fit Perfectly
Most applicants in the automation space only know zero-code tools (Zapier/Make) and crumble when enterprise APIs hit rate limits or require custom logic. 
* **Your Edge:** Your ability to write **Python** automation scripts natively gives you complete control over complex data transformations. 
* **The Secret Sauce:** Calling **LLM APIs** to process inputs is easy, but enterprise systems fail without operational resilience. Your knowledge of **error handling backoffs (exponential retry loops)** means your scripts don’t crash when OpenAI/Anthropic return `429 Rate Limit` or `503 Service Unavailable` errors. Pair that with returning structured **JSON** to downstream apps and tracking script iterations in **Git**, and you are immediately more reliable than 80% of applicant pools.

### 🔍 Live Search Phrases to Use on Indeed / LinkedIn
* `("Python") AND ("LLM" OR "OpenAI API") AND ("Automation") AND NOT ("PhD")`
* `"AI Automation Engineer" remote`
* `("Python") AND ("JSON") AND ("API") AND ("retry" OR "backoff")`

### 2. Applied AI Data Operations Engineer

### 💼 Job Title & Average Market Rate
* **Target Titles:** Data Operations Engineer, AI Data Wrangler, Unstructured Data Specialist
* **Average Market Rate:** **$32 – $48/hour** ($70,000 – $100,000/year)

### 🎯 Why Your Skills Fit Perfectly
Companies are sitting on mountains of unstructured text (PDFs, support tickets, emails) and need it converted into structured, database-ready formats.
* **Your Edge:** You leverage **LLM APIs** not for fancy chat interfaces, but as processing engines to parse raw text into clean, deterministic **JSON** schema outputs. 
* **The Secret Sauce:** Processing millions of tokens via API requires robust batching. Your explicit knowledge of **error handling and backoff mechanics** guarantees that data pipelines process gracefully without data loss during network hiccups. Using **Git/GitHub** to manage your data pipeline repositories demonstrates standard software engineering discipline, elevating you above non-technical data analysts.

### 🔍 Live Search Phrases to Use on Indeed / LinkedIn
* `("Python") AND ("Data Extraction") AND ("OpenAI" OR "Anthropic")`
* `("Data Operations" OR "Data Ops") AND ("Python") AND ("JSON") AND ("Remote")`
* `("Python") AND ("LLM") AND ("Structured Data" OR "JSON")`

---

### 3. Technical AI Solutions Engineer (Integration Specialist)

### 💼 Job Title & Average Market Rate
* **Target Titles:** AI Integration Engineer, Solutions Engineer (AI Products), Technical Implementation Specialist
* **Average Market Rate:** **$38 – $52/hour** ($80,000 – $110,000/year)

### 🎯 Why Your Skills Fit Perfectly
SaaS platforms are rushing to add "AI features" to their products. They need mid-level engineers who can glue third-party AI endpoints onto their legacy software products.
* **Your Edge:** You understand the end-to-end flow of modern API integration: pulling data, structuring payload inputs in **JSON**, passing them securely via **Python** to **LLM APIs**, and handling the responses.
* **The Secret Sauce:** Production integrations live and die by edge cases. Demonstrating that you proactively build defensive code—handling timeout exceptions, API failure modes, and using **exponential backoff logic**—proves to an engineering manager that your code won't bring down their staging or production environments. **GitHub** portfolio proof of these retry-resilient integrations makes hiring you a low-risk decision.

### 🔍 Live Search Phrases to Use on Indeed / LinkedIn
* `("Integration Engineer" OR "Solutions Engineer") AND ("Python") AND ("LLM" OR "API")`
* `("Python") AND ("REST API") AND ("Git") AND ("JSON") AND ("AI")`
* `"AI Integration Specialist" remote`
